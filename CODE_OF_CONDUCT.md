# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

GameArchiveManager welcomes respectful, constructive participation. Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- discuss ideas and technical disagreements without personal attacks;
- respect different experience levels, backgrounds, languages, and accessibility needs;
- give actionable feedback and accept correction in good faith;
- protect other people's privacy and confidential security information;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, spam, and deliberate disruption.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release coordination, and other public or private spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, repetition, and the safety of affected participants.

To report conduct that should not be discussed publicly, use a private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. If none is available, open a minimal issue requesting private contact and omit sensitive details. Reports will be handled as privately as the available channel permits.

Good-faith security research and respectfully stated criticism are not violations of this policy.
